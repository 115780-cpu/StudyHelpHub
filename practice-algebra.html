➕ Algebra Practice

Practise your algebra skills by solving the questions below. Try each question yourself before clicking "Show Answer" to check your work.

💡 Study Tip

Always show your working when solving algebra problems. Work through each step carefully and check your answer by substituting your value back into the original equation.

📝 Practice Question 1

Solve:

3x + 7 = 22

Working:

3x + 7 = 22

3x = 22 − 7

3x = 15

x = 5

Answer: x = 5

📝 Practice Question 2

Simplify:

5a + 3a − 4

Working:

5a + 3a − 4

8a − 4

Answer: 8a − 4

📝 Practice Question 3

Expand:

3(x + 4)

Working:

3(x + 4)

= 3x + 12

Answer: 3x + 12

📝 Practice Question 4

Factorise:

x² + 7x + 12

Working:

We need two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7.

3 × 4 = 12

3 + 4 = 7

(x + 3)(x + 4)

Answer: (x + 3)(x + 4)

📝 Practice Question 5

Solve:

5y − 10 = 40

Working:

5y − 10 = 40

5y = 50

y = 10

Answer: y = 10

📝 Practice Question 6

Simplify:

4x + 2x − 3 + 5

Working:

4x + 2x − 3 + 5

6x + 2

Answer: 6x + 2
