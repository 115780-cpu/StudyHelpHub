➕ Arithmetic

Arithmetic is the foundation of mathematics. It involves working with numbers using the four basic operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. Arithmetic skills are used every day in shopping, budgeting, cooking, measuring, and solving everyday problems.

📚 What You'll Learn

  • Addition
  • Subtraction

  • Division
  • Fractions
  • Decimals
  • Percentages
  • Order of Operations (BODMAS/PEMDAS)

💡 Study Tip

Always check your calculations carefully and remember the order of operations (BODMAS/PEMDAS). Practising mental maths regularly can improve both your speed and accuracy.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

Calculate:

458 + 276

Answer

458 + 276 = 734

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

Solve using the correct order of operations:

8 + 4 × 5

Answer

Multiply first:

4 × 5 = 20

8 + 20 = 28
