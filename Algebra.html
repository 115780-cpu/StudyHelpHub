➕ Algebra

Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses letters and symbols to represent numbers and solve problems. It is one of the most important topics because it forms the foundation for many other areas of mathematics.

📚 What You'll Learn

  • Variables
  • Expressions
  • Equations
  • Factorising
  • Expanding Brackets
  • Linear Equations
  • Quadratic Equations

💡 Study Tip

Always simplify expressions and show each step of your working. Writing each step clearly makes it easier to spot mistakes and understand how equations are solved.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

Solve:

2x + 5 = 17

Answer

2x = 12

x = 6

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

Simplify:

4a + 3a - 2

Answer

7a - 2

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

Expand:

(x + 4)(x + 2)

Answer

x² + 6x + 8

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

Factorise:

x² + 9x + 20

Answer

(x + 4)(x + 5)

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

Solve:

5y - 10 = 40

Answer

y = 10
