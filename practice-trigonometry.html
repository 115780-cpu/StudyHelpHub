📏 Trigonometry Practice

Practise your trigonometry skills using the questions below. Try to solve each problem yourself before clicking "Show Answer".

💡 Study Tip

Remember the mnemonic SOH CAH TOA to choose the correct trigonometric ratio. Always identify the opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse before solving a problem.

📝 Practice Question 1

Find the missing side.

A right-angled triangle has an angle of 30° and a hypotenuse of 10 cm. Find the length of the opposite side.

Working:

sin(30°) = opposite ÷ hypotenuse

0.5 = opposite ÷ 10

opposite = 5 cm

Answer: 5 cm

📝 Practice Question 2

Find the missing side.

A right-angled triangle has an angle of 60° and an adjacent side of 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

Working:

cos(60°) = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse

0.5 = 8 ÷ hypotenuse

hypotenuse = 16 cm

Answer: 16 cm

📝 Practice Question 3

Find the missing side.

A right-angled triangle has an angle of 45° and an adjacent side of 10 cm. Find the opposite side.

Working:

tan(45°) = opposite ÷ adjacent

1 = opposite ÷ 10

opposite = 10 cm

Answer: 10 cm

📝 Practice Question 4

Find the missing angle.

In a right-angled triangle, the opposite side is 6 cm and the hypotenuse is 12 cm. Find the angle.

Working:

sin(θ) = opposite ÷ hypotenuse

sin(θ) = 6 ÷ 12

sin(θ) = 0.5

θ = 30°

Answer: 30°

📝 Practice Question 5

Find the missing angle.

A right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 5 cm and an adjacent side of 5 cm. Find the angle.

Working:

tan(θ) = opposite ÷ adjacent

tan(θ) = 5 ÷ 5

tan(θ) = 1

θ = 45°

Answer: 45°

📝 Practice Question 6

Find the missing side.

A right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of 10 cm and an angle of 60°. Find the adjacent side.

Working:

cos(60°) = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse

0.5 = adjacent ÷ 10

adjacent = 5 cm

Answer: 5 cm
