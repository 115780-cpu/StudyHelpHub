🌍 Earth Science

Earth Science is the study of our planet, including its rocks, minerals, atmosphere, oceans, weather, climate and natural processes. Explore how Earth changes and how its different systems interact.

💡 Study Tip

When studying Earth Science, connect processes together. For example, plate movement can cause earthquakes and volcanic activity, while the water cycle connects the atmosphere, land and oceans.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

What are the three main layers of Earth?

Answer

The three main layers are the crust, mantle and core.

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

What causes tectonic plates to move?

Answer

Tectonic plates move because of heat-driven movement within Earth's mantle, including convection and forces acting at plate boundaries.

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

What is the process called when liquid water changes into water vapour?

Answer

The process is called evaporation.

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

What type of rock forms when magma or lava cools and solidifies?

Answer

It forms an igneous rock.

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

What instrument is used to measure earthquake vibrations?

Answer

A seismometer is used to detect and measure ground vibrations caused by earthquakes.
