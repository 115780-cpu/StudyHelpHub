💡 Study Tip

Always write down the information you know, choose the correct formula, substitute the values, and include the correct units in your final answer.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?

Answer

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

A force of 20 N acts on an object with a mass of 5 kg. Find its acceleration.

Answer

F = ma

a = F ÷ m

a = 20 ÷ 5

4 m/s²

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

An object has a mass of 4 kg and is moving at 5 m/s. Find its kinetic energy.

Answer

KE = ½mv²

KE = ½ × 4 × 5²

KE = 2 × 25

50 J

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

A wave has a frequency of 5 Hz and a wavelength of 2 m. Find its speed.

Answer

v = fλ

v = 5 × 2

10 m/s

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

An object has a mass of 10 kg. What is its weight on Earth? Use g = 9.8 m/s².

Answer

Weight = mass × gravitational field strength

W = 10 × 9.8

98 N
