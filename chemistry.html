⚗️ Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter, its properties and how substances interact with one another. Explore atoms, elements, compounds, reactions and the changes that happen to substances around us.

💡 Study Tip

Learn the symbols and properties of common elements. Drawing particle diagrams can also help you understand atoms, compounds and chemical reactions more easily.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

What is the chemical symbol for oxygen?

Answer

The chemical symbol for oxygen is O.

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

How many protons does a carbon atom have?

Answer

Carbon has 6 protons.

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

Is water (H₂O) an element or a compound?

Answer

Water is a compound because it contains hydrogen and oxygen chemically bonded together.

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

What pH value is considered neutral?

Answer

A neutral substance has a pH of 7.

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

Name the three common states of matter.

Answer

The three common states of matter are solid, liquid and gas.
