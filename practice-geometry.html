📐 Geometry Practice

Practise your geometry skills by solving the questions below. Try each question yourself before clicking "Show Answer" to check your work.

💡 Study Tip

Draw a diagram whenever possible and label all the information given in the question. This can make it much easier to identify the correct formula and solve the problem.

📝 Practice Question 1

Find the missing angle.

A triangle has angles of 60° and 70°. What is the third angle?

Working:

180° − 60° − 70° = 50°

Answer: 50°

📝 Practice Question 2

Find the area of a rectangle.

Length = 12 cm
Width = 8 cm

Working:

Area = Length × Width

Area = 12 × 8

Area = 96 cm²

Answer: 96 cm²

📝 Practice Question 3

Find the perimeter of a rectangle.

Length = 10 cm
Width = 6 cm

Working:

Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width)

= 2 × (10 + 6)

= 32 cm

Answer: 32 cm

📝 Practice Question 4

Find the hypotenuse.

A right-angled triangle has sides of 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the length of the hypotenuse.

Working:

6² + 8² = c²

36 + 64 = 100

c = √100

c = 10 cm

Answer: 10 cm

📝 Practice Question 5

Find the circumference.

A circle has a radius of 7 cm. Use π ≈ 3.14.

Working:

Circumference = 2 × π × r

= 2 × 3.14 × 7

= 43.96 cm

Answer: 43.96 cm

📝 Practice Question 6

Find the sum of the interior angles.

Find the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon.

Working:

(n − 2) × 180°

(6 − 2) × 180°

4 × 180° = 720°

Answer: 720°
